How these prompts are built

  • One subject, one action. Short clips cannot hold several actions. If you need more, make a second scene.
  • One camera move, named physically. "Slow push-in" or "drone fly-over", not "dynamic camera". See the camera movements guide.
  • Shot size and angle first. They decide the framing. See camera angles and shot sizes.
  • Light and style set the look. One light source and one style beat a long list of adjectives.
  • No text, no logos, no brand names. Models draw letters badly. Add them in editing.

Each prompt below is written by the Promptoo tool from the options listed under it, so what you copy here is exactly what the builder produces. Prompts are a starting point: models vary, and the same prompt can give different results on each run.

AI video prompt examples: frequently asked questions

What makes a good AI video prompt?

One clear subject, one main action, a place, a shot size, one camera move, a light source and a style. Each example on this page follows that pattern, and avoids text, logos and several actions at once, which models handle badly.

Do these prompts work in Veo, Sora, Kling, Runway, Luma and Pika?

They are written in plain, physical language that these text-to-video tools generally understand, so they are a good starting point in any of them. Results differ between models and from run to run, so treat each as a base to adjust. Promptoo is not affiliated with these tools.

Can I use these as AI image prompts for Midjourney or Stable Diffusion?

Yes. The image examples have no camera movement because a still has none. For video examples, drop the motion lines and keep the subject, setting, shot, lens, light and style.

How long should an AI video prompt be?

Usually 40 to 120 words is enough. Longer prompts tend to add instructions the model drops. Keep the details that change the picture and cut the rest.

Why do the examples avoid text and logos?

AI video and image models usually draw letters wrongly. Add text later in an editor. The examples also keep to one subject and one action, which is the most reliable way to get a clean result.

Can I use these prompts commercially?

The prompts are free to copy and adapt. What you may do with the generated video or image depends on the terms of the tool you use. Promptoo itself is free for personal use, and commercial use of the Promptoo tool needs permission from Avan Labs.

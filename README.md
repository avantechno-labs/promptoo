# Promptoo

**Build better AI video and image prompts, scene by scene, with a live 3D preview.**

Promptoo is a free prompt builder for AI video and image generators such as Google Veo, OpenAI Sora, Kling, Runway, Luma, Pika and Midjourney. Instead of guessing the right words, you pick the shot, camera move, lens, light and style, see them in a 3D preview, and copy a clear, structured prompt.

## Why Promptoo

AI video models respond to the language of film: shot sizes, camera angles, lens choices, lighting. Most people do not know those words, and a vague prompt gives a vague video. Promptoo gives you the vocabulary as pictures you can click, warns you when your choices fight each other, and writes the prompt for you.

## What you can do

- **Build scene by scene.** Describe who is in the scene and what they do, then add the place, the shot, the camera and the look. Chain scenes into one prompt with timings.
- **See it before you generate.** A live 3D preview shows your camera move, lens, light, weather, location and style with a stand-in character, so you can fix framing before you spend credits.
- **Choose from over 300 options.** 10 shot sizes, 21 camera angles, 45 camera movements, lenses, lighting, moods, color grades, 39 locations, 46 visual styles, weather, seasons, eras and crowds.
- **Direct the movement.** Set what the character does, how fast and in which direction, what moves in the background, and who moves relative to the camera.
- **Get warnings that help.** A built-in checker explains conflicting choices, for example a locked-off camera with a push-in, or "standing still" with "running".
- **Start from an example.** Browse copy-ready prompts for cinematic scenes, product shots, nature, anime and more, and open any of them in the builder.
- **Keep characters consistent.** Define a character once and reuse the same description in every scene.
- **Save and reuse.** Keep scenes, characters and whole projects in your library, and export or import them as a file.
- **Polish with AI (optional).** Rewrite a prompt using your own API key and compare the original with the rewrite side by side. Works with Anthropic, OpenRouter (including free models), Groq, Gemini, local models and any OpenAI-compatible service.
- **Extend it.** Every 3D effect is a plug-in, so new camera moves, lights and styles are easy to add.

## Private by design

Your prompts, scenes and templates stay in your browser, on your device. There is no account and no server for your projects. If you use the optional AI rewrite, your prompt goes to the provider you choose, using your own key.

## Who it is for

Filmmakers, creators, marketers, designers and anyone making AI video or images who wants more control and fewer wasted generations.

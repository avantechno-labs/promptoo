Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

The short version: Promptoo runs in your browser. There are no accounts and no server for your projects. We do not sell your data. Your prompts leave your device only if you choose to send them to an AI provider with the optional Rewrite with AI button.

Who we are

Promptoo is created and run by Avan Labs (avanlabs.com). You can reach us through the contact page.

What Promptoo stores on your device

Promptoo saves the following in your browser's local storage, on your own device:

  • your project: scenes, picks, characters and settings;
  • your template library, custom styles and custom effects;
  • preferences such as the theme and the 3D preview switch;
  • your AI provider choice, and your API key only if you tick "remember". The key is stored unencrypted in your browser, so do not tick it on a shared computer.

We do not receive this data. It is removed when you clear your browser data or use the Reset button. Use Export to keep a backup file.

Rewrite with AI (optional)

If you use this feature, your browser sends the prompt text, your extra instruction and the checker's list of problems directly to the AI provider you selected, together with your API key. We never see this data or your key. The provider's privacy policy and terms apply to it. If you choose a local model such as Ollama or LM Studio, the text goes to your own computer.

What we collect

Promptoo itself does not collect personal data. It has no accounts, no sign-up and no forms that send data to us. At the time of writing, Promptoo uses no analytics, advertising or tracking scripts, and it does not set cookies. If we add privacy-friendly analytics in future, we will update this page first.

Third parties

  • Google Fonts. Our pages load fonts from Google Fonts, so Google receives your IP address and browser details when a page loads, as described in Google's privacy policy.
  • Hosting. Our web host may keep standard server logs (such as IP address, page requested and time) to run and secure the site.
  • Links. Links to other sites, such as avanlabs.com and AvanLink, are covered by their own policies.

Your choices

  • Clear your data at any time with Reset in the tool or your browser's site-data settings.
  • Export and delete your project whenever you like.
  • Do not use Rewrite with AI if you do not want to send text to an AI provider.

Children

Promptoo is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest update.

Contact

For privacy questions, use the contact page.

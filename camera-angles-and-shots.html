Camera Angles and Shot Sizes for AI Video and Image Prompts

Shot size is how much of the subject fills the frame. Camera angle is where the camera sits relative to the subject. Together with a camera move they describe a shot. This guide covers the 10 shot sizes and 21 angles in Promptoo. They work for AI video and for still images.

Quick answer: pick a shot size first (how big the subject is), then an angle (where the camera is), then optionally a move. Describe the angle geometrically, for example "camera below the subject's eyeline, pointing upward".

Shot sizes (10)

Extreme wide shot

Tiny subject in a huge place.

Prompt wording: Extreme wide shot, the subject a tiny figure in the vast scene

Wide establishing shot

Show the whole place first.

Prompt wording: Wide establishing shot, the full scene visible and the subject small in frame

Wide shot

Whole body with lots of surroundings.

Prompt wording: Wide shot, the subject's whole body with plenty of the surroundings visible

Full shot

Head to toe, body language.

Prompt wording: Full shot, the subject seen head to toe

Medium wide shot

From the knees up, the cowboy shot.

Prompt wording: Medium wide shot, the subject framed from the knees up

Medium shot

Waist up, conversation and action.

Prompt wording: Medium shot, subject framed from the waist up

Medium close-up

Chest up, emotion with context.

Prompt wording: Medium close-up, subject framed from the chest up

Close-up

Face fills the frame, feelings.

Prompt wording: Close-up on the subject's face

Extreme close-up

One detail, eyes or an object.

Prompt wording: Extreme close-up, one detail filling the frame

Insert shot

A tight detail of an object.

Prompt wording: Insert shot, a tight detail of one object filling the frame

Height (10)

Eye-level angle

Neutral, natural, like a person looks.

Prompt wording: Eye level, the camera at the subject's natural eye height looking straight at them

Low-angle shot

Makes the subject look powerful.

Prompt wording: Low angle, the camera below the subject's eyeline pointing upward

High-angle shot

Makes the subject look small or weak.

Prompt wording: High angle, the camera above the subject pointing downward

Bird's-eye view

Far above, a map-like view.

Prompt wording: Bird's-eye view, the camera far above the subject looking almost straight down

Overhead top-down shot

Straight down from directly above.

Prompt wording: Overhead top-down, the camera directly above the subject with the lens pointing vertically down

Hip-level angle

Waist height, grounded and a little heroic.

Prompt wording: Hip level, the camera at waist height looking slightly up

Shoulder-level angle

Just below eye height, natural handheld feel.

Prompt wording: Shoulder level, the camera just below eye height for a natural, grounded view

Ground-level angle

Lens on the ground, floor in front.

Prompt wording: Ground level, the camera almost touching the ground with floor surfaces in the foreground

Worm's-eye view

From the ground looking steeply up.

Prompt wording: Worm's-eye view, the camera on the ground looking steeply upward at the subject

Hero angle

Iconic, confident, a little low.

Prompt wording: Hero angle, the camera slightly below the subject aimed upward for a powerful, iconic frame

Tilt (3)

Dutch angle

Tilted horizon, unease.

Prompt wording: Dutch angle, the camera rolled so the horizon runs diagonally

Extreme dutch angle

Very tilted, chaos or madness.

Prompt wording: Extreme Dutch angle, the camera heavily rolled so the horizon is sharply diagonal

Upside-down angle

World flipped, disorientation.

Prompt wording: Upside-down angle, the camera rotated 180 degrees so the world is inverted

Side and view (8)

From-behind angle

Follow someone into the scene.

Prompt wording: From behind, the camera directly behind the subject facing the same way

Profile angle

Side view of the subject.

Prompt wording: Profile, the camera at the subject's side seeing them in side view

Three-quarter angle

Flattering, about 45 degrees off front.

Prompt wording: Three-quarter angle, the camera about 45 degrees off the subject's front

Reverse angle

Look back from the opposite side.

Prompt wording: Reverse angle, the camera on the opposite side of the previous shot looking back

Point-of-view shot

Seen through the character's eyes.

Prompt wording: First-person point of view, seeing exactly what the character sees

Over-the-shoulder shot

Dialogue between two people.

Prompt wording: Over the shoulder, the camera behind one character's shoulder looking toward another

Single shot

One character alone in frame.

Prompt wording: Single, one character alone in the frame

Two-shot

Two characters framed together.

Prompt wording: Two-shot, both characters framed together

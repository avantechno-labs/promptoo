AI Video Camera Movements: 45 Moves and How to Prompt Them

AI video models respond to camera language. This guide lists the 45 camera movements in Promptoo, what each one does, when to use it and how to word it so the model understands. Every move can be tried in the live 3D preview.

Quick answer: describe what the camera physically does, what stays fixed, and where it ends. Use one main move per scene. Say "no lens zoom" if you mean a dolly, and "locked-off, zero camera movement" if you want a still camera.

How to write a camera move in an AI video prompt

  • Be physical. "Camera pushes slowly forward toward her face" works better than "dynamic camera".
  • One main move. Add a second only if the two form one clear, physical shot. Stacked, unrelated moves give conflicting instructions.
  • Dolly is not zoom. A dolly moves the camera through the scene. A zoom changes only the lens. Name the one you want.
  • Combine with a shot size and an angle. For example "low-angle medium shot with a slow push-in".
  • Let the subject move when the camera is still. If the camera is locked off, the subject carries the motion.

Pan / Tilt (6)

Static locked-off camera

No movement, the subject carries the shot.

Prompt wording: The camera holds perfectly still on a locked-off frame

Pan right

Scan a room or follow action to the right.

Prompt wording: The camera pans right

Pan left

Scan a room or follow action to the left.

Prompt wording: The camera pans left

Tilt up

Reveal height, look up a tall subject.

Prompt wording: The camera tilts up

Tilt down

Look down, drop to a detail.

Prompt wording: The camera tilts down

Whip pan

Violent snap between subjects, energy.

Prompt wording: The camera whip-pans quickly

Zoom (6)

Slow zoom in

Gentle creeping focus on the subject.

Prompt wording: The camera zooms in slowly with the lens, the camera itself staying still

Slow zoom out

Gently widen to show context.

Prompt wording: The camera zooms out slowly with the lens, the camera itself staying still

Fast zoom in

Sudden attention, a retro 70s feel.

Prompt wording: The camera zooms in quickly with the lens, the camera itself staying still

Fast zoom out

Sudden reveal of the wider scene.

Prompt wording: The camera zooms out quickly with the lens, the camera itself staying still

Crash zoom in

Shock or comedy emphasis.

Prompt wording: The camera snaps in with a sudden, violent zoom onto the subject

Crash zoom out

Shock reveal.

Prompt wording: The camera snaps out with a sudden zoom to reveal the wider scene

Dolly / Track (9)

Slow push-in

Build intensity, move toward a face.

Prompt wording: The camera slowly pushes in toward the subject

Slow pull-out

Reveal context, show isolation.

Prompt wording: The camera slowly pulls back to reveal the surroundings

Tracking shot

Travel with the subject.

Prompt wording: The camera tracks alongside the subject

Steadicam follow

Smooth follow, we are right behind them.

Prompt wording: The camera follows the subject with a smooth steadicam

Side tracking shot

Walk-and-talk from the side.

Prompt wording: The camera travels alongside the subject at the same speed, holding a steady medium-wide frame

Vehicle tracking shot

Cars, bikes, trains in motion.

Prompt wording: The camera tracks beside a moving vehicle at its speed, keeping it framed

Chase shot

Urgent pursuit.

Prompt wording: The camera pursues the subject closely from behind at speed, keeping the frame tight

Reverse tracking walk-and-talk

Face-forward walking conversation.

Prompt wording: The camera moves backward ahead of the subject, keeping them framed as they walk toward the lens

Low tracking shot

Feet, speed, ground detail.

Prompt wording: The camera tracks close to the ground alongside the subject with the lens near floor level

Truck, Pedestal, Arc (12)

Orbit around subject

Circle the subject, heroic or dreamy.

Prompt wording: The camera orbits smoothly around the subject

Orbit clockwise

Orbit with a known direction.

Prompt wording: The camera circles the subject at a fixed distance, travelling clockwise, smooth and continuous

Orbit counterclockwise

Orbit the other way.

Prompt wording: The camera circles the subject at a fixed distance, travelling counterclockwise, smooth and continuous

Arc left

Short curved move, a new view of the subject.

Prompt wording: The camera curves in a partial arc to the left around the subject, ending at a new side angle

Arc right

Short curved move, a new view of the subject.

Prompt wording: The camera curves in a partial arc to the right around the subject, ending at a new side angle

Truck left

Sideways travel, reveals parallax.

Prompt wording: The camera slides sideways to the left, parallel to the subject, without turning

Truck right

Sideways travel, reveals parallax.

Prompt wording: The camera slides sideways to the right, parallel to the subject, without turning

Slider left

Subtle product and detail move.

Prompt wording: The camera glides a short distance left on a slider, slow and smooth

Slider right

Subtle product and detail move.

Prompt wording: The camera glides a short distance right on a slider, slow and smooth

Pedestal up

Rise to a new height, level view.

Prompt wording: The camera rises straight up on a vertical column without tilting

Pedestal down

Drop to a new height, level view.

Prompt wording: The camera lowers straight down on a vertical column without tilting

Push past pass-by shot

Fly past something to a reveal.

Prompt wording: The camera pushes forward and passes close beside the subject in one sweep

Handheld (1)

Handheld shaky cam

Raw, urgent, documentary feel.

Prompt wording: The camera moves handheld with a slight natural shake

Drone / Crane (6)

Crane up

Rise to reveal the scene below.

Prompt wording: The camera cranes upward

Crane down

Come down into the scene.

Prompt wording: The camera descends smoothly on a crane toward the subject

Drone fly-over

Aerial sweep over a landscape.

Prompt wording: The camera flies over the scene like a drone

Drone pull back

Reveal how big the place is.

Prompt wording: The camera pulls straight back and upward from the subject, revealing the setting

Drone push in

Fly toward a landmark or person.

Prompt wording: The camera pushes in toward the subject from above and ahead

Helicopter shot

Big cinematic aerials.

Prompt wording: The camera sweeps high above the landscape in one broad, smooth aerial pass

Specials (5)

Dolly zoom

Vertigo effect, the background warps.

Prompt wording: The camera performs a dolly zoom, the background stretching behind the subject

Snorricam body-mounted camera

Dizzy, subjective, panicked.

Prompt wording: The camera is rigidly mounted to the subject's body, so their face stays fixed while the world moves behind them

Pass-through-objects shot

Transition-style continuous move.

Prompt wording: The camera flies through an opening or object in one continuous move into the next space

Earth zoom out

Epic scale reveal.

Prompt wording: The camera pulls back continuously from the subject out to a view of the whole planet

Infinite zoom

Endless nested scenes.

Prompt wording: The camera keeps pushing deeper into the image as each layer becomes a new scene
